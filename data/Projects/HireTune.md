HireTune AI came out of a problem that nearly every student job seeker knows too well: tailoring your resume for every application is exhausting, but skipping that step usually makes the application weaker. We built HireTune at **HackUSF 2025** as a way to help applicants produce stronger, more targeted submissions faster, without spending hours manually rewriting the same resume for every role.

The platform combined a **React** frontend with a **Django** backend and the **OpenAI API** to generate ATS-aligned resume drafts in under **10 seconds**. We also built in live AI edits so users could refine specific sections quickly, then added a **LaTeX-based PDF pipeline** for one-click export into consistent, polished templates. That combination made the product feel much more useful than a simple text generator.

We pushed the project further by adding **Discord OAuth** and a built-in job tracker, so users could manage different tailored resume versions per role instead of treating each application as a disconnected document. Built in **36 hours** with a 4-person team and containerized with **Docker**, HireTune felt like a real product MVP rather than just a hackathon prototype.

## Highlights

- **ATS-aligned resume generation** in under 10 seconds using React, Django, and the OpenAI API
- **Live AI editing plus 1-click PDF export** through a LaTeX-based document pipeline
- **Discord OAuth and job tracking** for managing tailored applications per role
- **Production-shaped MVP** shipped in 36 hours with Dockerized setup and demo readiness
